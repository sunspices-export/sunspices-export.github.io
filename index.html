Our Heritage

The Vandanmedu Mists

Our Alleppey Green Extra Bold (AGEB) cardamom is nurtured in the mist-filled valleys of Vandanmedu, where rich organic soils and cool-temperate climate are ideal. Every pod is hand-picked at precisely 11:00 AM — when essential oils peak — ensuring vibrant emerald hue and intense aroma that global buyers demand.
